I'm Hung Phan, a first-year Ph.D. student at the [National University of Singapore](https://www.nus.edu.sg/).
My research lies at the intersection of **large language models**, **optimization**, and **operations research**.
I build LLM-based agents and search frameworks for complex optimization problems, particularly combinatorial ones,
and study how optimization methods can in turn strengthen the reasoning, search, and decision-making of language-model agents.

Previously, I received my B.Eng. in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence from [Hanoi University of Science and Technology](https://hust.edu.vn/) with Highest Distinction,
where I worked with Prof. Huynh Thi Thanh Binh and Dr. Nguyen Thi Tam on multi-objective and dynamic vehicle routing.
I was also a research assistant at OLab, VinUniversity, with Asst. Prof. Chu Thi Mai Hong.

**Research interests:** LLM agents & long-horizon planning · Multi-objective optimization · Combinatorial optimization.
